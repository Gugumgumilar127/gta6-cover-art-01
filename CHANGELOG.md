# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Palm Heat Cover Studio

- Multi-panel cover composer: Cast of Five, Hero + Three, Trio and Solo layouts
  for landscape, square and portrait posters.
- Four colour grades (Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour, Noir) with warm washes,
  vignette and film grain, adjustable per cover.
- Chrome sunset title lettering with a striped sheen, neon script subtitle,
  letterspaced tagline pill and slanted cast labels that never hide under the
  title block.
- Magazine furniture drawn from the cover's own words: five-star review quote,
  volume chip, credits strip, seeded barcode and the original-art seal.
- Photo handling stays on the visitor's machine: drag to reframe, scroll to
  zoom, export a share-ready PNG at 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080.
- Six bundled sample portraits, free to use, so the first cover is already cast.
